### Winter Aquathlon 1

- Enjoyed how casual the event was
- Went out really hot on the run with the group and had to drop back fast.
- After 1 lap looked down at my watch and saw my heart rate was 183 bpm and thought "that's not good"
- Finished the run at a good pace but was pretty gassed with my heart rate in the 180's the whole time
- T1 went well and got in the water
- First 1.5 laps I was just fighting for my life to recover my heart rate and catch my breath.
- The next 5 laps I settled in and found a good rhythm drafting off the guy in front of me
- For the last 2.5 laps I picked up the pace a bit
- Bit of a slow T2 as I had to put socks on (didn't want to risk blisters)
- Slower run but I had enough gas in the tank to pick up my pace in the last bit which I thought was a good sign
- This guy Shae Kelly (I'm sure you know him) was there and totally smoked me by 10 min in my age bracket haha. No worries I was running my own race.

### Winter Aquathlon 4

- Less nervous than other ones.
- Nice chat with guys before
- Went out on the run. One guy just absolutely blitzed out at the start and then was quickly swallowed up by the pack. I ran near the back with 2 other guys and ended up leading them out to transition. My heart rate was pinned at 180 basically the whole time.
- Got in the water quick from the transition passing a guy who was ahead of me (who later passed me again)
- got in the water and didn't feel quite as bad as I expected to. Gave myself the first 3 laps to calm down and get into a 3 stroke pattern but I never got there. By about lap 7 I was mixing a good chunk of breathing every 3 but still not fully comfortable in that. My arms felt like lead for the first 7 laps. In the last few laps I felt energized and picked up the pace a bit. Couldn't find any feet to draft on b/c everyone else was way off my speed.
- Slow transition 2 because I had to sit on my butt and pull socks over wet feet. Also went up the stairs really slow
- Got on run course and was gassed. Ran solo most of it and was just trying to hold a pace I could manage. Picked it up for the last two laps.

### Beat the base

- Really well run
- Nearly missed check in and bike drop off because it was a late start to the day and I didn't pay too much attention to how early I had to show up
- It was a hurry up and wait situation though because they were behind on the pool
- I was first of four swimmers but was quickly passed and stayed in second passing others a few times.
- Overall a fast swim b/c it is a 800 yards instead of 750 m
- Out to T1 with my heart pumping. Passed the guy who got out of my lane first with a quick transition. Opted to sit on my butt b/c that always feels faster
- Bike was pretty nice. A few life ending pot holes too dodge but overall pretty good. Biking the airstrip felt like a headwind both ways. Occasional odd pockets where it would open up and feel like there was no wind. My hamstrings were clenching fierce and were my limiting factor. Spent about as much time in aerobars as I could hope to
- T2 was good but felt like crap running my bike in
- Got out on run course and settled into what my body naturally wanted to run which was about 4:30/km. For the first 2 km I wasn't sure I could hold it but then I got far enough that I resolved to just push through and hold it. Sprinted through to the finish line. Beat 1:25:00 time so I "beat the base".
